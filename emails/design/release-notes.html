Your canvas.
More possibilities.

A closer connection between your ideas, your tools and the interfaces you ship. Here’s what’s new in Plastic.

01

A cover that is yours.

Choose any frame as your file thumbnail. The same design appears on your dashboard and in shared-link previews.
02

Real interfaces. Real source.

Design visually with semantic HTML and CSS. Inspect the source, edit styles, and keep the original cascade, tokens, fonts and assets.
03

Bring your Figma files.

Import .fig files with pages, layers, images, text, design tokens and auto layout remaining editable.
04

A canvas that stays out of your way.

Use rulers and guides, smarter text creation, native copy and paste, layer dragging, and clearer Fit, Fixed, Fill and Relative sizing.
05

Build once. Use it everywhere.

Create reusable components, keep instances in sync, and refine responsive styles and interaction states directly in the editor.
06

Your agents, on the canvas.

Connect local coding agents through MCP. Read a selected frame with its full context and see which layers an agent is reading or editing.
07

Files that keep up.

Automatic saves, external-change sync, cloud file storage and live editor updates keep your work connected across tools.
08

Polish in the details.

A refreshed chrome identity, quiet loading states, AI activity indicators, smoother layer navigation and refined inspector controls.
